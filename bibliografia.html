Fertilidad — Ovulación, progesterona y trigger

  1. Practice Committee of the American Society for Reproductive Medicine. Use of clomiphene citrate in infertile women: a committee opinion. Fertil Steril. 2013;100:341-348.

    Aporte: mayores tasas de embarazo con folículo líder de 23-28 mm en ciclos con clomifeno.

  2. Practice Committee of the American Society for Reproductive Medicine. Use of exogenous gonadotropins for ovulation induction in anovulatory women: a committee opinion. Fertil Steril. 2020;113:66-70. https://www.asrm.org/practice-guidance/practice-committee-documents/use-of-exogenous-gonadotropins-for-ovulation-induction-in-anovulatory-women-a-committee-opinion-2020/

    Aporte: gonadotropinas en dosis bajas y cancelación del ciclo con más de dos folículos mayores de 16 mm.

  3. Palatnik A, Strawn E, Szabo A, Robb P. What is the optimal follicular size before triggering ovulation in intrauterine insemination cycles with clomiphene citrate or letrozole? An analysis of 988 cycles. Fertil Steril. 2012;97:1089-1094. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22459633/

    Aporte: límite de tolerancia de 28 mm con clomifeno o letrozol.

  4. Shalom-Paz E, Marzal A, Wiser A, Hyman J, Tulandi T. Does optimal follicular size in IUI cycles vary between clomiphene citrate and gonadotrophins treatments? Gynecol Endocrinol. 2014;30(2):107-110. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24266698/

    Aporte: disparo con clomifeno desde 20 mm.

  5. Ling L, et al. Effect of follicle size on pregnancy outcomes in patients undergoing first letrozole-intrauterine insemination. Eur J Med Res. 2024;29:184. https://eurjmedres.biomedcentral.com/articles/10.1186/s40001-024-01794-8

    Aporte: óptimo de 19,1-21 mm con letrozol.

  6. Van Oirschot M, et al. Optimal follicle size for human chorionic gonadotropin trigger in mono-follicular growth during letrozole-induced intrauterine insemination cycles: findings from a large retrospective study. Fertil Steril. 2025. https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S001502822500158X

    Aporte: rango de 19-23 mm con letrozol.

  7. ESHRE Ovarian Stimulation Guideline Group. Ovarian stimulation for IVF/ICSI: guideline update 2025. https://www.eshre.eu/-/media/sitecore-files/Guidelines/COS/2025/ESHRE-OS-guideline-updateSept-2026v23.pdf

    Aporte: el tamaño de disparo se individualiza.

  8. ESHRE Guideline Group on Female Infertility. Female infertility: a guideline. Hum Reprod Open. 2023;2023(3):hoad034.

    Aporte: monitoreo ecográfico seriado en la inducción de ovulación.

  9. Ministerio de Salud Pública y Bienestar Social del Paraguay. Res. S.G. N.º 050/2025.

    Aporte: día de ovulación y ventana del dosaje de progesterona.

Preconcepcional — Panel hormonal

  1. Practice Committee of the American Society for Reproductive Medicine. Testing and interpreting measures of ovarian reserve: a committee opinion. Fertil Steril. 2020. http://www.asrm.org/practice-guidance/practice-committee-documents/testing-and-interpreting-measures-of-ovarian-reserve-a-committee-opinion-2020/

    Aporte: FSH basal y AMH como marcadores de reserva ovárica.

  2. Alexander EK, et al. 2017 Guidelines of the American Thyroid Association for the diagnosis and management of thyroid disease during pregnancy and the postpartum. Thyroid. 2017;27:315-389.

    Aporte: meta de TSH menor de 2,5 en la etapa preconcepcional.

  3. Guía CaVeSal® «Perfil hormonal femenino en fertilidad» (Historia Clínica CaVeSal®). https://historia.cavesal.app/guias-clinicas

    Aporte: cortes de FSH, LH/FSH, AMH, prolactina y progesterona del día 21.

Infecciones genitales bajas

  1. Workowski KA, Bachmann LH, Chan PA, et al. Sexually Transmitted Infections Treatment Guidelines, 2021. MMWR Recomm Rep. 2021;70(4):1-187. https://www.cdc.gov/std/treatment-guidelines/default.htm

    Aporte: criterios de vaginosis, candidiasis complicada y recurrente, tricomoniasis y esquemas orales y locales, con ajustes en el embarazo.

  2. Amsel R, Totten PA, Spiegel CA, Chen KC, Eschenbach D, Holmes KK. Nonspecific vaginitis: diagnostic criteria and microbial and epidemiologic associations. Am J Med. 1983;74:14-22. https://doi.org/10.1016/0002-9343(83)91112-9

    Aporte: 3 de 4 criterios clínicos.

  3. Nugent RP, Krohn MA, Hillier SL. Reliability of diagnosing bacterial vaginosis is improved by a standardized method of Gram stain interpretation. J Clin Microbiol. 1991;29:297-301. https://doi.org/10.1128/jcm.29.2.297-301.1991

    Aporte: puntaje 0-3, 4-6 y 7-10.

  4. Donders GG, Vereecken A, Bosmans E, Dekeersmaecker A, Salembier G, Spitz B. Definition of a type of abnormal vaginal flora that is distinct from bacterial vaginosis: aerobic vaginitis. BJOG. 2002;109:34-43. https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1471-0528.2002.00432.x

    Aporte: puntaje de vaginitis aeróbica y su gravedad.
